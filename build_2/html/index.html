BLOCKHEAD
On 28th February 6:15 AM UTC, shortly before sunrise, American and Israeli forces launched the first strikes of Operation EPIC FURY and Operation Roaring Lion.
Hundreds were killed on the first day as munitions rained down on Iran. Among the dead, Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, who had ruled the Islamic republic without reprieve since 1989.
In the days that followed, defence officials spoke of hacked traffic cameras and compromised cell towers in Tehran. The strike which killed Khamenei was an intelligence coup which had taken years of top-secret planning.
However, it did not come as a surprise for everyone. 8,000 miles away near London, a different type of killing had taken place. One hour before the strike, on Amazon's EU-West-2 servers, trading on Polymarket's CLOB servers spiked abruptly.
A small group of wallets, many of them with no trading history, had placed well-timed bets on an Iran war starting on the 28th, just hours before the first missiles were launched.
One of the biggest winners was Vivaldi007, a 'fresh wallet' created just 20 days ago. The account made US$700,000 by correctly predicting the day of the strike, the successful assasination of Khamenei as well as Israel's close participation.
Despite its expcetional foresight, Vivaldi007 had no interest in making a second set of wagers. A total of $400,000 was transferred out on the same day, while the remainng would be transferred out on the 3rd March.
Like many of its peers, the wallet has been silent since.

Long bets, defined as wagers of more than $2500 and under the median value of the position, were place by just {255} unique wallets. 63 of them had no prior history of trading.
During the last 12 hours before the strike, they made a combined total $1.7 million in profits. Approximately 32% of all profits in the market.
For some, the timing had been extremely fortunate. 0x45647..., an account with no prior history of trading, took a long position just 2 hours before the strike.

Another account by the name of Dicedicedice would go even further. Between 00:00 and 01:00 AM, it would buy 149,995 YES shares at $0.20 per share.
